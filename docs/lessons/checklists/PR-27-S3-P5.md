## D-PRE-2 resolution — rationale for option (b)

The S2 architecture surface 3 options for the verdict-aggregator
amendment placement :
- **(a)** : new subsection number under § 24 (e.g., § 24.3.6 if a
  S-suite verdict-aggregator section existed)
- **(b)** : add the amendment directly under § 27 (CB-suite) and
  reference S-suite by symmetry
- **(c)** : reorganise § 24 to give the S-suite verdict aggregator
  its own first-class section

**Pass 5 binôme decision** : option (b).

**Rationale** :
1. Option (a) requires inventing a subsection (§ 24.3.6) under § 24
   which is actually the Bench public-page surface.  This is a category
   error : public-page artefacts vs verdict aggregator logic are
   distinct concerns.
2. Option (c) is structurally cleaner but is a STABILITY-bump touching
   the whole § 24 numbering.  Out of scope for PR #27 (which is G.11
   closure, not contract reorganisation).
3. Option (b) is pragmatic : the CB-suite already has § 27 dedicated
   to the catalogue, and the S-suite shares the verdict aggregator
   logic (same `compare::compare()` code path).  Symmetric reference
   is appropriate.

**Final mapping** :
- § 27.2.5 — closed taxonomy `tolerance_used.unit` (added by PR #27)
- § 27.2.6 — closed taxonomy `tolerance_used.direction` (added)
- § 27.2.7 — closed taxonomy `tolerance_used.source` (added)
- The `tolerance_used.shape` contract is documented in the same § 27.2
  area (no separate § 24.3.6 needed).  S-suite inherits by reference.

**Action** : the § 24.9 PENDING row body is updated to explicitly
reference "the verdict-aggregator amendment lives under § 27.2.5/6/7
per Pass 5 binôme decision" (applied in fix-in-S3 patch below).
